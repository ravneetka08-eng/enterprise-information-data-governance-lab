# 04 — Controls and compliance mapping

This is an educational mapping, not legal advice. Applicable obligations depend on the university's legal status, record class and jurisdiction. Confirm current Victorian public records requirements, the Privacy and Data Protection Act 2014 (Vic), the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) where relevant, applicable Commonwealth Privacy Act obligations, contractual terms and approved Public Record Office Victoria disposal authorities with qualified staff.

| Control | Risk addressed | Owner | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Named library/site owner | Orphaned information | Business owner | Asset register completeness |
| Least-privilege groups | Excessive access | IT + Data Owner | Negative access test |
| Metadata standard | Poor discoverability | Steward | Required metadata review |
| Version history | Accidental overwrites | Site owner | Edit and inspect versions |
| Classification and sharing guidance | Disclosure | Governance + privacy | Sharing test and review |
| Access approval record | Unauthorised access | Data Owner | Ticket/decision linkage |
| Approved retention and legal-hold check | Premature destruction | Records Manager | Authority and hold evidence |
| Quarterly permissions review | Privilege creep | Data Owner | Review sign-off |
| Ticket escalation | Unresolved complex cases | Service desk | Escalation record |

**Do not treat a demo retention interval as a real disposal authority.**
