# 03 — Draft MDU information governance standard

**Scope:** fictional MDU staff information in Microsoft 365. **Status:** proposed for review, not approved institutional policy.

## Principles
- Assign a Data Owner and Steward to each governed dataset and document collection.
- Use minimum necessary access and group-based permissions where practical.
- Use approved information architecture and metadata conventions; avoid uncontrolled deep folders.
- Distinguish working copies, official records and personal working files.
- Apply classification and handling requirements based on sensitivity.
- Record access decisions, exceptions, expiry and periodic recertification.
- Retain records only under an authorised schedule; do not destroy on a guessed timeframe.
- Check legal holds and disposal authority before any irreversible action.
- Maintain evidence of staff guidance, audits, incidents and remediation.

## Classification (illustrative)
- Public: authorised for publication.
- Internal: routine university work, limited to staff or permitted partners.
- Confidential: student, employee, financial or commercially sensitive information.
- Highly Restricted: exceptional high-impact information; require documented approval and stronger controls.

## Sharing rules
Internal sharing defaults to specific authorised users. External sharing requires a legitimate purpose, an approved recipient and relevant privacy/security review. Review link scope, expiry and access rights. Do not assume a sensitivity label alone overrides all SharePoint permissions.

## Lifecycle
Create/capture -> classify -> store and describe -> use/share -> review -> retain -> authorised dispose/transfer with evidence.

## Ownership
Data Owner: accountable and approves access; Data Steward: definitions, controls and coordination; IT: implements technical permissions; Records Manager: retention authority; Privacy Officer: privacy advice.
