# 06 — Staff guidance and service desk examples

## Staff guide: where should a document go?
- Personal draft: OneDrive, with appropriate sharing and transfer to institutional storage when it becomes an official record.
- Departmental working document: governed SharePoint library, with an owner and suitable metadata.
- Team collaboration: Teams channel with awareness that channel files usually reside in SharePoint.
- Formal official record: follow the Records Manager's approved capture and retention process; a SharePoint file is not automatically a registered EDRMS record.

## Sample ticket: wrong sharing permission
**Issue:** A synthetic HR file was shared to an overly broad internal group.
**Triage:** Classify as potential confidentiality risk; limit exposure using authorised procedure and notify appropriate owners.
**Escalation:** IT security/privacy officer if exposure may have occurred; do not unilaterally erase evidence.
**Resolution:** Correct permissions after approval, confirm least privilege, document who approved change, record follow-up training.

## Sample ticket: metadata confusion
**Issue:** Staff cannot find final finance policies.
**Resolution:** Explain approved library views and metadata values, correct misclassified synthetic files with owner approval, update staff guidance.
